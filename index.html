About me

I studied psychology, and everything I make comes back to it in some way. Expert Voice is about the trust you build by being yourself. Echo is about wellbeing and being heard, which is what my degree was in. The Grad Groundwork came from finishing uni, watching friends with clear paths, and realising how hard starting a career is when the traditional route doesn't fit you. Seam is the practical one: websites that stitch everything a business does into one place, so it flows.

They look like four different things. To me they're the same habit: notice a problem I care about, then build the thing that fixes it.
